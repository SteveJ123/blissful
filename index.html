This Program Includes

Four Guided Practices for Body, Mind & Spirit

Each 60-minute evening session weaves together four gentle, time-tested disciplines designed specifically for women and men at any fitness level.

01 · Vocal Resonance

Chanting

Mindful rhythmic chanting gently slows the nervous system, eases evening mental chatter, and anchors your awareness in calm presence.

Focus: Mental clarity & nervous system calm

02 · Subtle Joint Mobilization

Sukshma Vyayama

Gentle, low-impact yogic micro-movements targeting the neck, shoulders, wrists, spine, and ankles. Safe and accessible for every age group, Sukshma Vyayama dissolves stiffness from desk work or household routines and restores effortless mobility.

Focus: Joint flexibility, posture relief & gentle circulation

03 · Radiance & Lymphatic Flow

Face Yoga Exercises & Massages

Targeted upward toning exercises paired with soothing fingertip facial massages. Relieves clenched jaw and forehead tension, reduces puffiness through natural lymphatic drainage, and nurtures a healthy, refreshed glow without harsh products.

Focus: Natural facial toning, jaw relaxation & healthy glow

04 · Vital Energy & Breath

Prana Awakening

Guided pranayama and subtle vital-energy awareness practices to replenish depleted reserves, balance emotions, and support deeper, restful sleep.

Focus: Breath vitality & restful evening sleep
